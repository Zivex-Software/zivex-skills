## Conditional additions

- **Healthcare (for example NEN 7510)**: patient data is special-category; log every read and write of patient data with its purpose (NEN 7513); NEN 7512 for exchanges; strong authentication for care providers; a DPIA is mandatory.
- **Government (for example BIO2)**: a baseline on ISO 27002:2022; check the national "comply or explain" standards list (for example government profiles for OAuth and OpenAPI); an accessibility statement on the government register is mandatory.
- **EU AI Act**: classify the use (prohibited, high-risk, limited, minimal); transparency to users for AI interaction and generated content; human oversight; logging of AI decisions; no training on client data without a contract.
- **MASVS (apps)**: storage, crypto, auth, network, platform, code, resilience, privacy; L1 baseline, L2 for sensitive apps.
