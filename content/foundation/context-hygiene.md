# Context hygiene

- Read only what the task needs: the module's `AGENTS.md`, the files you will change, and their direct callers and tests.
- Search before you read: find the symbol or file, then open that part, instead of opening whole directories.
- Do not dump build or test output into the conversation; look for the failing lines and summarise them.
- When a task grows beyond its original scope, say so and agree on the new scope instead of silently expanding.
